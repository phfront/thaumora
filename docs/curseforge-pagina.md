## Descrição (Description)

```markdown
# Thaumora

**A magic-only adventure for Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge).**

The world was once ruled by schools of magic that destroyed each other. You start as an apprentice
with no mana, exploring ruins, books and abandoned towers, and slowly master every school.

## The rules of Thaumora

- **No electricity.** No RF/FE, no cables, no Mekanism, Thermal, AE2 or Refined Storage.
  Storage, automation, travel and combat are solved with spells, spirits, rituals and alchemy.
- **Create is the only machinery**, treated as *arcane engineering*: mechanical, visible, manual.
  No electric Create add-ons.
- **Free progression.** Nothing is locked. There is a suggested path through five schools, but you
  can go wherever you want.
- **Made for singleplayer.**

## The five schools (suggested path)

| Tier | School | Main mods |
|---|---|---|
| I | Naturalism: herbs, potions, cauldron witchcraft | Hexerei, Hexalia, Enchanted: Witchcraft, Roots Classic, Botania (early) |
| II | Arcanism: glyphs, custom spells, Source | Ars Nouveau and add-ons, Forbidden & Arcanus, Thaumaturge |
| III | Occultism: demons, spirits, souls | Occultism, Malum, Eidolon: Repraised, Embers |
| IV | Battle magic: combat spell schools, deep enchanting | Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks, Apotheosis |
| V | Forbidden magic: blood, sacrifice, legendary artifacts | Neo Vitae, EvilCraft, Reliquary, Botania endgame |

## Magical automation instead of tech

- **Storage:** Occultism Dimensional Storage
- **Autocrafting:** Create 6 logistics, Ars Nouveau Wixies, Botania Corporea
- **Transport and farming:** Starbuncles, spirits, Nature's Aura, Mystical Agriculture

## World

Twilight Forest, The Aether + Deep Aether, Eternal Starlight, Undergarden, Deeper and Darker,
Bumblezone, a reworked Nether (Incendium) and End (Nullscape, Unusual End, End Remastered),
Tectonic terrain, YUNG's structures, When Dungeons Arise, L_Ender's Cataclysm and Mowzie's Mobs bosses.

## Requirements

- **RAM:** 10 GB recommended (8 GB minimum)
- Shaders work (Iris + Sodium are included); try Complementary Reimagined.

## Beta notes

- This is a **beta**: configs are still the mods' defaults and there is **no quest book yet**.
- **Third-party launchers (Prism, ATLauncher...):** *Structory* and *Structory: Towers* don't allow
  downloads outside the CurseForge app. The launcher will ask you to download those two files
  manually. The CurseForge app installs everything automatically.
```
